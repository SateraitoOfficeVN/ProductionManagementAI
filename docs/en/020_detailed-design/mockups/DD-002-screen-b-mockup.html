Scope. Static mockup, not a prototype: nothing here is interactive. It fixes layout, state coverage and the visual treatment of status, the overdue marker and the sort indicator; behavior is specified in DD-002 and DD-002-SPD.

Data. Sample rows follow DB-003's seed shape — 80 orders across all four statuses and 30 products, with due dates spread around the run date so some are overdue. Order numbers, product names and timestamps are illustrative.

Read-only. No state-changing control appears anywhere on this screen: every change to an order happens on Screen A (DEC-003).
